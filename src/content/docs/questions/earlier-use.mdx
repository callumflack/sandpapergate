## The claim and its scope

The relevant event is actual match use at Port Elizabeth. Sandpaper in a kit bag, practice on a training ball, and ordinary ball maintenance are different propositions.

This page presents archive analysis based on the cited sources. It distinguishes what a witness says, what an institution found and what can be inferred from either.

## Arguments for the claim

### Khawaja’s original report alleges the earlier start

The November 7 report attributes Port Elizabeth discussion and use to Khawaja’s book; he later stands by what he saw and the conversations he had there. This is the initiating allegation, distinct from Bancroft’s later details.

**Limit:** The newspaper’s event sequence is reporter narration of the memoir, not the acquired chapter. The reported 2018 notes are not captured or authenticated.

### Bancroft supplies a first-person account of using it

Bancroft describes taking sandpaper onto the field after tea on day two and using it when he collected the ball. He describes repeated use and feedback during the session. If accurate, this directly supports earlier use rather than merely a plan.

**Limit:** This is a 2026 recollection. Bancroft says his 2018 account omitted when the practice began; contemporary corroboration is still needed.

### Sayers describes a separate physical involvement

Sayers places his first awareness at Port Elizabeth and says sandpaper was handed to him to take off the field. That is a potentially corroborating act by another participant, beyond a general belief that everyone knew.

**Limit:** He says he assumes Bancroft handed it to him, cannot remember what he did with it afterwards, and qualifies his belief that it was not used again in that Port Elizabeth Test. Bancroft separately describes continuous use through the rest of that Test and Cape Town. Agreement on onset does not independently corroborate duration; preserve the disagreement and uncertainty.

## Arguments against or limiting the claim

### The contemporary public account said this was the first occasion

Smith told the March 2018 press conference this was the first time under his leadership. That is a contemporary account in tension with the later Port Elizabeth accounts.

**Limit:** Its reliability is itself disputed: CA subsequently charged Smith with misleading public comments. A contemporary statement is not automatically a truthful one.

### Cummins says the Port Elizabeth allegation was new to him

The captured Cummins coverage records his denial of knowing about sandpaper use at Port Elizabeth. It challenges the claim that the practice was common knowledge.

**Limit:** Lack of knowledge is not direct observation that the act never occurred. This is a challenge to shared knowledge, with less force against the narrower proposition that Bancroft used it.

## What would help resolve it

- Obtain and compare contemporary footage, any physical evidence and other first-person accounts specific to Port Elizabeth.
- Resolve Sayers’ limited recollection about when the sandpaper was removed and whether use continued.
- Do not infer the success or frequency of tampering from the number of wickets alone.
