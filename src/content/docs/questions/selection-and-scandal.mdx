## The claim and its scope

A participant’s concern about selection is not a demonstrated causal explanation of selection decisions. Assess each selection window, performance, role and alternative candidate rather than treating an entire career as one decision.

This page presents archive analysis based on the cited sources. It distinguishes what a witness says, what an institution found and what can be inferred from either.

## Arguments for the claim

### Bancroft describes a pattern he found difficult to explain

He points to strong domestic run-scoring periods, says opportunities did not materialise and wonders whether dressing-room tension after his 2021 comments played a part. This supplies his stated reason for suspecting a connection.

**Limit:** He explicitly acknowledges poor-form periods, competing candidates and cricketing reasons. His concern and statistics require independent checking and do not establish a selector’s motive.

## Arguments against or limiting the claim

### His own account recognises plausible cricketing explanations

Bancroft says selection is complicated, recognises times when he was out of form and notes decisions he was not part of. Those concessions mean the absence of a recall alone cannot isolate scandal-related exclusion as the cause.

**Limit:** These alternatives do not prove that the scandal had no influence. The evidence required is the contemporaneous reasoning for particular decisions, not an assertion that selection is always purely statistical.

### The selector’s reported explanation directly denies a political veto

The January 2024 report quotes Bailey rejecting any push from bowlers, saying the panel had not discussed Sandpapergate and explaining Green’s inclusion and Renshaw’s position. This supplies a specific competing explanation for a specific omission.

**Limit:** This is an AAP report with direct quotations, not the complete press conference or internal selection record. A denial is relevant evidence, not an independent audit; its strongest scope is the January 2024 decision discussed.

## What would help resolve it

- Acquire the complete selection press conference and contemporaneous selection record for each contested window; Bailey’s January 2024 quoted explanation is now collected.
- Check the domestic performance and role-comparison claims against scorecards and squad announcements.
- Avoid extending an explanation for one 2024 selection decision to every later decision.
