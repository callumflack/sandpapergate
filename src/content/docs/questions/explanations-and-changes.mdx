## The claim and its scope

That an explanation was inaccurate is different from who devised it, who knew it was false, who agreed and who later relied on it. An accusation of continuing coordination requires evidence beyond the original incorrect account.

This page presents archive analysis based on the cited sources. It distinguishes what a witness says, what an institution found and what can be inferred from either.

## Arguments for the claim

### The material explanation changed in an acknowledged way

The initial public account describes tape and granules. Bancroft’s 2026 statement explicitly says sandpaper was concealed behind that explanation and calls it another lie. CA’s charges also identify misleading public comments.

**Limit:** This strongly supports inaccuracy of the early account, but not by itself the identity or number of those who coordinated it.

### Bancroft alleges specific advice before the press conference

He identifies Saker as warning against naming sandpaper and says coaches, management and players discussed the response, with some agreeing and others disagreeing. This is an allegation of coordination beyond the two speakers.

**Limit:** The account is retrospective and does not identify every participant or supply their exact responses. It needs the named staff responses and contemporaneous corroboration.

## Arguments against or limiting the claim

### The early account does not itself identify a wider cover-up group

Bancroft described panic when seen on the screen, while Smith publicly assigned the original plan to a leadership group and excluded coaches. Neither establishes who proposed the later tape story beyond the people who spoke.

**Limit:** Panic and wider coordination can coexist; the early account’s failure to identify others is not proof others were uninvolved.

### Silence at a post-exposure meeting does not establish earlier agreement

Bancroft recalls nobody professing ignorance at the subsequent meeting and others advising how to minimise damage. By then sandpaper had been exposed; that silence cannot on its own establish prior knowledge or a prior pact.

**Limit:** This challenges the inference from that meeting, rather than refuting the specific allegation of pre-conference advice.

## What would help resolve it

- Verify the published original Q&A against recordings and obtain complete relevant staff accounts.
- Which proposed statements were agreed before the first press conference, and by whom?
- Treat later inconsistency, an admitted omission and a proven continuing agreement as different findings.
