## The claim and its scope

Warner’s involvement and whether it was exclusive are separate propositions. A team benefit or permissive culture does not by itself establish collective planning or absolve the person who proposed the method.

This page presents archive analysis based on the cited sources. It distinguishes what a witness says, what an institution found and what can be inferred from either.

## Arguments for the claim

### The public charges specifically attribute development and instruction to Warner

CA’s published account charges Warner with developing the plan, instructing Bancroft and demonstrating how to alter the ball. The 2024 review decision records Warner accepting the report’s description of his conversation and demonstration.

**Limit:** The 2024 proceeding concerned modification of a sanction, not a new investigation of everyone’s role. Acceptance of Warner’s involvement does not establish exclusivity.

### Bancroft’s later account still identifies Warner’s proposal

Bancroft says Warner raised sandpaper and explained why camera attention meant he could not carry it out personally. Even the account alleging wider involvement retains a concrete initiating role for Warner.

**Limit:** Bancroft frames it as happening within wider team conversations; acknowledging the proposal is compatible with others’ encouragement or prior knowledge.

### The complete Gilchrist interview directly names Warner

Asked who requested the action, Bancroft says Warner suggested it. The question and answer supply a direct attribution from Bancroft in December 2018, before his more expansive 2026 account.

**Limit:** The interview does not establish that Warner acted alone; it also records Bancroft’s choice and desire to fit in. Interview content must not be reduced to a headline assigning all responsibility to Warner.

## Arguments against or limiting the claim

### Scope limit: wider participation does not refute Warner’s initiating role

He recalls people agreeing, others offering views on when to act, and playing and coaching staff later acknowledging his role. If accurate, the organisational setting involved more than a private instruction between two players. This challenges exclusivity, not the proposition that Warner was a principal initiator.

**Limit:** Participation in discussion and collective benefit must still be distinguished from authorship of the plan, express approval and knowledge in each case.

### Corroboration limit: Sayers does not recall the origin

Sayers remembers discussion and Bancroft agreeing to act, but explicitly says he does not remember whose idea it was initially. His account cannot independently confirm the origin of the proposal.

**Limit:** Failure to remember an origin is not positive evidence that Warner did not propose it. It limits corroboration rather than refuting the specific allegation.

### Erskine alleges wider instructions and protection of others

Warner’s manager says more than three were involved, says he advised Warner to protect colleagues, and alleges earlier instructions by executives in Hobart. These claims raise a possible institutional context beyond a single player’s initiative.

**Limit:** These remain a manager’s allegations, with no established basis that he witnessed the Hobart exchange. The executives are unnamed and a 2016 allegation does not itself establish a 2018 sandpaper instruction. Full audio and an automatic draft are now collected; the bowlers commentary after the guest farewell belongs to the host, not Erskine. Wider instructions would not themselves disprove Warner’s own initiating role.

## What would help resolve it

- What does the original investigation evidence establish about proposal, agreement and demonstration separately?
- Does anyone identify another initiator with direct rather than second-hand knowledge?
