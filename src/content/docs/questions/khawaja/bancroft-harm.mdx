<span class="claim-kind">Continuing harm; distinct from a proven selection veto</span>

## What Khawaja alleges

Khawaja says Bancroft has not regained his place despite scoring runs and that the scandal hangs over him. He presents this as part of the injustice to the three.

**Source boundary:** This does not itself allege a specific bowler veto or establish a causal explanation for every selection decision.

## Strongest case for the claim

### Bancroft describes continuing concern, and selectors acknowledge his runs

Bancroft’s later account expresses concern about dressing-room tensions and missed opportunities. Bailey’s account acknowledges excellent Shield performance and a difficult 2024 selection decision. This makes Khawaja’s concern concrete rather than resting on no sporting case.

**Limit:** Bancroft’s selection suspicions are his later account, not a more specific allegation that can silently be attributed to Khawaja. Strong output alone cannot identify a selector’s motive.

## Strongest challenge or limit

### The 2024 selector gives competing cricketing reasons

Bailey denies a political or bowler veto and explains the preference for Green in the best six, Smith opening and Renshaw next in line. Bancroft himself acknowledges poor-form periods and competing candidates.

**Limit:** This is the selector’s stated account, not an independent audit; it addresses a particular selection window, not every later opportunity or the full extent of stigma.

## Where the record stands

Continuing stigma has firsthand support. A stronger claim that the scandal caused a particular non-selection remains decision-specific and weakly established by runs or suspicion alone.

This is an assessment of the captured record, not an adjudicated finding.

## Open questions

- Which selection window and role are being challenged?
- What were the alternatives, fitness considerations and contemporaneous reasons?
- What evidence could separate reputational stigma from a causal selection exclusion?
