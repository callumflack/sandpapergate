## Strongest case for the claim

### Bancroft recalls a similar response

Bancroft says Smith entered the conversation and distanced himself, and recalls a further similar response during the match. Separately, CA’s charges identify Smith’s knowledge of a potential plan and failure to prevent it at Cape Town.

**Limit:** The CA charge supports Cape Town knowledge and failure to prevent, not independent corroboration of the earlier Port Elizabeth date or remembered words.

## Strongest challenge or limit

### Distancing does not settle either authorship or adequate intervention

The later accounts distinguish Smith’s response from the proposal and instructions attributed to Warner. Wanting no part in a plan need not mean authorising it, while a captain’s withdrawal need not discharge a duty to stop it.

**Limit:** This is a limit on the inference, not a captured denial that the earlier conversation occurred. The archive has no complete contemporary record of Smith’s Port Elizabeth response.

## Where the record stands

Cape Town knowledge and failure to prevent are specified in CA’s public charges. The earlier exchange has later participant support but remains unverified against a contemporary record. Neither operational authorship nor effective intervention follows from distancing alone.

This is an assessment of the captured record, not an adjudicated finding.
