## What Khawaja alleges

The original report attributes this intervention to Khawaja’s book.

**Source boundary:** The report’s view that the conference worsened the scandal is retrospective narration. The attempt to delay it and the predicted benefit of delay are separate claims.

**Read the allegation:** [SP055 · ‘It was all of us’: Khawaja blows the whistle on more sandpaper use in South Africa in 2018](/documents/sp055-usman-khawaja-s-original-november-2026-newspaper-interview/): [p-013](/documents/sp055-usman-khawaja-s-original-november-2026-newspaper-interview/#p-013)

## Strongest case for the claim

### Bancroft also recalls disagreement about speaking immediately

Bancroft remembers some people wanting to wait until the following day and Smith wanting to address the matter immediately. That is consistent with a disagreement about timing.

**Limit:** These passages do not identify Khawaja as the person urging delay. They corroborate the general disagreement rather than every part of his account.

**Read the passages:** [SP031 · Cameron Bancroft’s full 4500-word Sandpapergate statement](/documents/sp031-bancroft-long-form-statement-original-publication/): [p-142](/documents/sp031-bancroft-long-form-statement-original-publication/#p-142) · [p-143](/documents/sp031-bancroft-long-form-statement-original-publication/#p-143) · [p-144](/documents/sp031-bancroft-long-form-statement-original-publication/#p-144) · [p-145](/documents/sp031-bancroft-long-form-statement-original-publication/#p-145)

## Strongest challenge or limit

### The precise intervention and its benefit remain unverified

The captured accounts do not establish the exact alternative Khawaja proposed or show that a delay would have produced a more truthful or less damaging statement. Bancroft’s separate allegation about coordinating a false material explanation is an additional claim.

**Limit:** There is no captured named denial of Khawaja’s intervention. An absence of contemporary confirmation must not be presented as affirmative contrary testimony.

## Where the record stands

Partly consistent later testimony supports a timing disagreement. Khawaja’s specific role has limited corroboration; any claim that waiting would have improved the outcome is counterfactual.

This is an assessment of the captured record, not an adjudicated finding.

## Open questions

- Who proposed waiting, and what did they propose saying instead?
- Was the purpose to obtain facts, legal advice, a common account or something else?
