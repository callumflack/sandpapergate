## What Khawaja alleges

Khawaja says he was not interviewed despite sitting beside Bancroft. He calls the process insufficiently thorough and rushed. The original report also describes time pressure and a Cape Town focus.

**Source boundary:** His non-interview is a first-person factual claim; the quality of the whole investigation is a broader judgment. The reporter’s account of remit is not the underlying terms of reference.

## Strongest case for the claim

### Other relevant evidence may also have been missed or withheld

Sayers says he expected an interview and was not interviewed despite his own claimed involvement. Bancroft admits omitting material timing and knowledge information from the process. These are concrete reasons to question exhaustiveness.

**Limit:** They do not establish the full witness list or prove investigators knowingly suppressed evidence. Bancroft’s concealment could contribute to an incomplete result without institutional intent.

## Strongest challenge or limit

### CA describes a thorough process; other participants were interviewed

The public releases defend the investigation’s thoroughness. Cummins says he was interviewed and recalls requests for other information. Saker also describes participating. These accounts show an investigative process, rather than no inquiry.

**Limit:** They do not explain the alleged omissions or establish that every relevant witness was sought. An invitation to volunteer is not automatically sufficient investigation of an obvious witness.

## Where the record stands

The claimed non-interviews raise a substantial procedural question. The completeness judgment cannot be settled from public assurances or later recollections alone; remit, witness selection and treatment of conflicting evidence matter.

This is an assessment of the captured record, not an adjudicated finding.

## Open questions

- What were the exact remit, witness list, interview questions and evidentiary standards?
- Why were Khawaja and Sayers allegedly not approached?
- What opportunities to volunteer evidence existed, and what was actually supplied?
