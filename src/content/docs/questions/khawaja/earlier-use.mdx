## What Khawaja alleges

Brettig’s November 7 report attributes this earlier onset to Khawaja’s book, locating first use on day two. In the ABC interview Khawaja stands by what he saw and the conversations he had at Port Elizabeth.

**Source boundary:** The newspaper contains a reporter’s summary of the book, not the complete first-person chapter. Khawaja’s reported 2018 notes are not in the archive.

## Strongest case for the claim

### Two other participants describe direct involvement

Bancroft describes taking sandpaper onto the field after tea on day two and using it. Sayers places his first awareness at Port Elizabeth and describes sandpaper being handed to him to take off the field. Those accounts support actual use and handling, beyond an inference from reverse swing.

**Limit:** These are distinct participant accounts, but all emerged publicly years later. Their independence in every detail is not established. Sayers assumes, rather than distinctly recalls, who handed it to him.

## Strongest challenge or limit

### The contemporary first-occasion account conflicts with the new chronology

Smith’s immediate March 2018 press conference presented the incident as the first occasion under his leadership. That is a contemporaneous account against the later onset narrative.

**Limit:** CA later charged Smith with misleading public comments. Contemporaneity alone does not make that account reliable. Cummins’s lack of earlier knowledge also cannot prove that earlier use never occurred.

### Agreement on onset does not establish continued use

Bancroft describes continued use through Port Elizabeth and Cape Town; Sayers qualifies his belief that it was not used again in that Port Elizabeth Test. This prevents treating their accounts as mutually confirming every part of the chronology.

**Limit:** Sayers’s limited recollection is not a categorical observation of non-use. This disagreement bears on duration more than on their common account of an earlier start.

## Where the record stands

Earlier onset has comparatively substantial participant support in this record. It remains a retrospective account without captured contemporary physical or broadcast confirmation. Duration is more disputed than onset.

This is an assessment of the captured record, not an adjudicated finding.

## Open questions

- Do the reported 2018 notes record the timing, and can their provenance be established?
- Which innings and sessions involved actual use, removal or reuse?
- Is there contemporary footage or an independently recorded observation of the Port Elizabeth use?
