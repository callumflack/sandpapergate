In *Sabr*, Usman Khawaja wrote that he believed race was the more important of two reasons Tim Paine was chosen over him as interim Test captain after Steve Smith stood down in 2018. He drew that conclusion from a remark the chairman of selectors reportedly made to a journalist. The other reason Khawaja gave was his role in the 2017 pay dispute.

[The passage from *Sabr*, quoted by AAP](https://aapnews.aap.com.au/news/test-skipper-finds-khawaja-s-racial-claims-most-serious) · [Khawaja answers Sarah Ferguson](/documents/sp024-khawaja-abc-7-30-interview/#p-049) · [Cummins responds at 29:22](/documents/sp070-pat-cummins-full-local-automatic-recognition-unverified-asr/#p-059)

## What Khawaja wrote and what the chairman reportedly said

Khawaja wrote that “they weren’t ready for a brown Australian cricket captain, even as an interim.” He said the chairman had discussed his suitability with a journalist Khawaja knew well. The chairman’s words, as Khawaja reported them, were “He doesn’t display the right stuff.” Khawaja took that to mean he did not fit the selectors’ idea of an Australian captain and had the wrong skin colour for the job. The words he attributed to the chairman make no mention of race. The link between those words and skin colour is Khawaja’s interpretation. [AAP’s account of the book passage](https://aapnews.aap.com.au/news/test-skipper-finds-khawaja-s-racial-claims-most-serious)

## What did he say when asked to substantiate it?

On 10 November 2026, ABC interviewer Sarah Ferguson asked how Khawaja had reached that conclusion. He acknowledged he had “no concrete evidence”. When she asked again whether race had played a part in this decision, he said he could never be certain. He described a career in which he had been stereotyped as lazy and said that, looking back, he thought choosing Paine was the right decision. [Khawaja’s first answer](/documents/sp024-khawaja-abc-7-30-interview/#p-050) · [His view of Paine’s appointment](/documents/sp024-khawaja-abc-7-30-interview/#p-053) · [Ferguson’s follow-up and his reply](/documents/sp024-khawaja-abc-7-30-interview/#p-054)

## Why did Cummins call it the most serious allegation?

At his 13 November press conference, Pat Cummins said this was the most serious thing he had read that week. He understood Khawaja to be saying that skin colour could rule someone out of the job Cummins now held. He rejected that suggestion and said an allegation of that gravity needed evidence. [Cummins’s answer](/documents/sp070-pat-cummins-full-local-automatic-recognition-unverified-asr/#p-059) · [What he said about evidence](/documents/sp070-pat-cummins-full-local-automatic-recognition-unverified-asr/#p-060)

Khawaja publicly attributed a racial motive to the people who chose Paine. The sources here give Khawaja’s account of the chairman’s remark. They contain no account from the journalist or the selectors confirming that race influenced the appointment.
