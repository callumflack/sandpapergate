## What Khawaja alleges

Ferguson introduces a categorical race explanation for the captaincy decision. Khawaja says he has no concrete evidence, describes how he felt, says he cannot be certain and does not necessarily attribute that particular decision to race. He also says Paine proved a good choice.

**Source boundary:** The interviewer’s premise must not be turned into Khawaja’s unqualified factual allegation. His broader experience and the causal explanation of one decision require different evidence.

## Strongest case for the claim

### His testimony supports the reported experience

Khawaja describes recurring stereotypes about laziness, fitting in and effort, and says he first felt fully part of the team during the 2022 Ashes celebration. That is direct testimony about his experience and the meaning he gives it.

**Limit:** Testimony to an experience does not independently establish the motives of the people making a particular appointment. No decision-maker document is captured here supporting a race-based veto.

## Strongest challenge or limit

### His own qualifications limit the particular causal claim

Khawaja explicitly acknowledges uncertainty and lack of concrete evidence, and retrospectively endorses Paine’s appointment. Cummins rejects a categorical racial restriction on captaincy.

**Limit:** Neither a general rejection of racism nor a good eventual appointment disproves a specific past bias. These limits do not refute Khawaja’s reported feelings or wider experience.

## Where the record stands

The interview establishes what Khawaja says he felt and the qualifications he makes. It does not establish discriminatory causation in the particular appointment. This question should remain separate from evidence about sandpaper knowledge.

This is an assessment of the captured record, not an adjudicated finding.

## Open questions

- If pursued, what is the appointment’s decision history and who made the decision?
- What evidence bears on those decision-makers’ reasons?
- Which broader experiences does Khawaja distinguish from that particular decision?
