These are archive analyses of specific propositions, not quotations of a source's opinion or verdicts. Each argument links to exact captured passages, states the inference being made and names its limits. The arguments do not receive equal weight merely because they appear on opposite sides.

- [Was sandpaper used before Cape Town?](/questions/earlier-use/). Sandpaper was used on the match ball in the second Test at Port Elizabeth, before the televised Cape Town incident.
- [Who knew, and when?](/questions/prior-knowledge/). Members of the bowling group knew about sandpaper being used on the match ball before the Cape Town television footage exposed it.
- [Did coaches know or encourage it?](/questions/coaches-awareness/). At least one member of the coaching staff knew about or encouraged match-ball tampering with sandpaper before it was publicly exposed.
- [Who devised and directed the plan?](/questions/warner-role/). Warner was a principal initiator of the sandpaper plan and instructed Bancroft to carry it out.
- [What did Smith know and do?](/questions/smith-role/). Smith knew about a potential sandpaper plan but distanced himself instead of authorising or directing its implementation.
- [Was the public explanation coordinated?](/questions/explanations-and-changes/). The inaccurate tape explanation and omission of wider involvement were coordinated beyond Smith and Bancroft.
- [Did the investigation settle who knew?](/questions/investigation-and-accountability/). The 2018 investigation’s public finding reliably establishes that only Smith, Warner and Bancroft had prior knowledge of the incident.
- [Were three players made to carry collective responsibility?](/questions/sanctions-and-responsibility/). The sanctions and public allocation of responsibility were unfair because a wider team and institutional failure was placed on three individuals.
- [Did the scandal affect Bancroft’s later selection?](/questions/selection-and-scandal/). Unresolved tensions around Sandpapergate contributed to Bancroft not being recalled after 2019.
- [Was Bancroft pressured into it?](/questions/bancroft-agency/). Bancroft’s junior status and pressure to belong materially constrained his choice to carry out the tampering.
