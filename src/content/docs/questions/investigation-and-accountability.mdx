## The claim and its scope

There is a published finding that prior knowledge was confined to three. Whether the available evidence justifies treating that finding as exhaustive is a separate question. Different reviews have different remits.

This page presents archive analysis based on the cited sources. It distinguishes what a witness says, what an institution found and what can be inferred from either.

## Arguments for the claim

### CA published a specific finding and individual charges

The reproduced official release expressly confines prior knowledge to three and differentiates their conduct. In 2021 CA said its integrity unit asked Bancroft for new information and he supplied none.

**Limit:** The underlying investigation report, interview schedule and complete evidence are not in this archive. An official conclusion and a later absence of supplied evidence are not substitutes for those records.

## Arguments against or limiting the claim

### An alleged relevant witness says he was never interviewed

Sayers describes helping remove sandpaper and says he expected an interview but was never interviewed. If accurate, his potentially relevant evidence was not directly tested in the original process.

**Limit:** The publisher transcript prints a speculative suggestion about knowing too much. The recording’s automatic editions do not independently settle that exact verb phrase, and in any event it is not evidence of investigator motive. His stated non-interview and the reason for it are separate propositions.

### Bancroft says he withheld material facts from investigators

His later statement admits that he did not give a complete truthful account and omitted timing and others’ awareness. If accepted, this directly limits the information on which the original process could rely.

**Limit:** An admission of earlier concealment also bears on evaluating his later credibility. It does not automatically make every new allegation true.

### Khawaja alleges both an omission and a reason for it

Khawaja’s original November 7 interview says he was not interviewed despite sitting beside Bancroft and explicitly alleges, as his belief, that investigators knew he wanted collective responsibility and would tell the truth. His later ABC answer describes a rushed process and stops short of calling it a cover-up. Both accounts must be preserved; non-interview, adequacy and intent are separate claims.

**Limit:** The claimed omission raises a procedural question but does not establish investigator motive. No captured witness-selection record explains the decision. The stronger original allegation must not be erased by the later qualification or presented as proven by the omission.

## What would help resolve it

- Obtain or establish the availability of the underlying report, interview list and contemporaneous witness accounts.
- What new evidence, rather than repeated allegation, has each later account supplied?
- Do not treat a cultural review, 2024 sanction modification or later private forum as a fresh factual reinvestigation.
- The original newspaper interview reports that Khawaja wrote notes in Johannesburg in 2018. Obtain the notes and their provenance before treating this report of their existence as contemporary evidence of their contents.
