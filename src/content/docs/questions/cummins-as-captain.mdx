Cummins’s current captaincy is why his response draws particular scrutiny now. He was a 24-year-old bowler at Cape Town in 2018. In November 2026 he spoke both as a witness to that tour and as the person leading the team today. What does he owe former teammates, the current team and the public in that role? [Cummins on speaking in both roles](/documents/sp026-13-november-cummins-press-conference-abc-live-record/#p-029)

## What he has said publicly

At his 13 November press conference, Cummins offered to stay until every question had been asked. He denied prior knowledge and involvement in a plan to use sandpaper. He said Khawaja was entitled to tell his story and criticised the way he had implicated teammates. Cummins also described a win-at-all-costs culture on the tour and spoke about his efforts to change it as captain. [Prepared remarks](/documents/sp026-13-november-cummins-press-conference-abc-live-record/#p-023) · [On team culture](/documents/sp025-13-november-cummins-press-conference/#p-030) · [Full press conference video](https://www.cricket.com.au/videos/4608514/pat-cummins-full-press-conference-ball-tampering-usman-khawaja-book-allegations-video)

Bancroft says the press conference prompted him to publish his own fuller account. It named Cummins in the on-field exchanges and described a Port Elizabeth team talk. Cummins’s manager, Neil Maxwell, said the press conference had already answered Bancroft’s allegations. The timing matters when asking which details Cummins has addressed in his own words. [Why Bancroft spoke](/documents/sp031-bancroft-long-form-statement-original-publication/#p-016) · [Bancroft’s recollection of the exchanges](/documents/sp031-bancroft-long-form-statement-original-publication/#p-077) · [Maxwell’s response](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-19/pat-cummins-maintains-position-on-sandpapergate/107402100)

## What people are asking of him

- **How he treats conflicting accounts.** Sayers said he had seen little of the press conference and thought Cummins’s remarks about Khawaja got unnecessarily personal. Cummins said Khawaja had been careless with teammates’ reputations. What would it look like for a captain to defend his teammates and give a fair hearing to people whose recollections differ? [Sayers on the press conference](/documents/sp029-15-november-sayers-interview-with-gerard-whateley/#p-081) · [Cummins on Khawaja](/documents/sp026-13-november-cummins-press-conference-abc-live-record/#p-027)
- **Whether he should continue as captain.** Ian Healy questioned the completeness of Cummins’s account and warned against rushing to remove him. Oliver Holt called for him to go. The question of fitness to lead may turn on what Cummins did in 2018, the accuracy of what he has said since, or how he handles the dispute now. [Healy’s interview](https://www.sen.com.au/news/2026/11/18/sandpaper-scandal-ian-healy-darren-lehmann-pat-cummins-legacy) · [ABC on calls to resign](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-19/sandpapergate-cameron-bancroft-article-makes-headlines-in-uk/107401658)

## What Cricket Australia can answer for

Cricket Australia backed Cummins’s leadership and announced a confidential forum for players and staff from the tour. It said the forum would hear accounts without imposing fresh sanctions and that the board would decide what to make public. What should Cummins do as a participant? What explanation does Cricket Australia owe as the body that ran the inquiry and controls what it releases? [Cricket Australia’s forum announcement](https://www.cricket.com.au/news/4610598)

## What remains open

- Should Cummins address Bancroft’s specific recollections publicly, in the forum, or both? Which details would make a further response substantive?
- What could warrant a correction or an apology for what was said in 2026?
- What evidence would justify asking him to stand down? Which part of that case depends on what can be established about 2018, and which part depends on his conduct as captain now?
