Cummins was a bowler playing his 13th Test at Cape Town. The question is whether he noticed the ball reversing, suspected deliberate alteration, knew specifically about the sandpaper, or knowingly encouraged its use. Each claim needs its own evidence. [Cummins on his role in 2018](/documents/sp026-13-november-cummins-press-conference-abc-live-record/#p-029)

## What was already on the record

Cricket Australia’s published 2018 finding confined prior knowledge of the Cape Town incident to Smith, Warner and Bancroft. Bancroft implied in 2021 that some bowlers knew, without naming anyone. Cummins, Starc, Hazlewood and Lyon said they learnt a foreign substance had been taken onto the field when they saw the Newlands footage on the big screen. ## What the November accounts add

- **Earlier use at Port Elizabeth.** Khawaja puts the onset in the previous Test. When asked directly about Cummins’s prior knowledge, he declined to answer. Sayers says he knew beforehand and was handed the sandpaper to take off the field. His belief that the frontline bowlers must have known comes from how the team managed the ball. Sayers doubts the sandpaper was used again in that Test, while Bancroft recalls repeated use. [Khawaja’s original account](/documents/sp055-usman-khawaja-s-original-november-2026-newspaper-interview/#p-005) · [Khawaja asked about Cummins](/documents/sp024-khawaja-abc-7-30-interview/#p-018) · [Sayers on handling the sandpaper](/documents/sp029-15-november-sayers-interview-with-gerard-whateley/#p-018) · [Sayers on the bowlers](/documents/sp029-15-november-sayers-interview-with-gerard-whateley/#p-078) · [Bancroft on repeated use](/documents/sp031-bancroft-long-form-statement-original-publication/#p-090)
- **Conversations involving Cummins.** Bancroft names Cummins among the bowlers he asked about reverse swing in both Tests. He recalls using more sandpaper after receiving feedback in Port Elizabeth. At Cape Town he says they told him his work on the ball was insufficient. He gives no words from Cummins explicitly acknowledging the sandpaper. [Port Elizabeth exchanges](/documents/sp031-bancroft-long-form-statement-original-publication/#p-077) · [Cape Town exchanges](/documents/sp031-bancroft-long-form-statement-original-publication/#p-127) · [Bancroft’s distinction between swing, suspicion and knowledge](/documents/sp031-bancroft-long-form-statement-original-publication/#p-134)
- **A Port Elizabeth team talk.** Bancroft recalls the sandpaper being discussed before warm-up on day three. If the method was stated aloud and Cummins was present, that would bear more directly on his knowledge than feedback about swing. Bancroft does not identify Cummins as having spoken or heard it at that talk. [Bancroft’s account of the team talk](/documents/sp031-bancroft-long-form-statement-original-publication/#p-099)

## What Cummins says

On 13 November, Cummins denied knowing sandpaper was being taken onto the field and said earlier use at Port Elizabeth was news to him. Asked about a huddle remark about the ball swinging, he said he did not recall it and explained why that sort of remark could arise during ordinary ball maintenance. After Bancroft’s account appeared, Cummins’s manager said he stood by the press conference and regarded the allegations as already answered. ## What remains open

- What did Bancroft and Cummins actually say to one another in each Test? What would show that they both understood the feedback to concern sandpaper?
- Was Cummins at the Port Elizabeth team talk? Who else recalls the method being named in his hearing?
- What did Cummins tell the 2018 investigators, and what did they ask? Cummins says he answered truthfully. Bancroft says he withheld material details. The archive is missing the original interviews.
