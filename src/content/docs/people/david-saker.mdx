## Three statements, different claims

- May 2019: [Saker regains passion as he eyes Melbourne Stars coaching job](/documents/sp075-saker-regains-passion-as-he-eyes-melbourne-stars-coaching-job/) contains his denial of condoning cheating and reflection on preventing the incident.
- May 2021: [David Saker’s response to Bancroft’s 2021 interview](/documents/sp064-bowling-coach-responds-to-bancroft-bombshell/) discusses a monumental mistake and wider blame without specifying who knew what.
- June 2021: [Bowling coach calls on Cricket Australia to release ball-tampering report](/documents/sp074-bowling-coach-calls-on-cricket-australia-to-release-ball-tampering-report/) expressly denies knowing sandpaper was involved.

These statements concern different burdens. Wider cultural responsibility is not an admission of specific knowledge; an assertion of not condoning cheating is not the same as not knowing. None is a full response to every allegation made in 2026.
