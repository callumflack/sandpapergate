## Accounts and responses

Bancroft’s later account names Marsh among the bowlers he spoke to about reverse swing and recalls Marsh commenting on it. [The named conversations](/documents/sp031-bancroft-long-form-statement-original-publication/#p-078) and [the recollected comment](/documents/sp031-bancroft-long-form-statement-original-publication/#p-092) are Bancroft’s allegations, not independent proof of Marsh’s knowledge.

In [‘I’ve moved on’: Marsh reveals Khawaja phone call in ball-tampering grilling](/documents/sp081-i-ve-moved-on-marsh-reveals-khawaja-phone-call-in-ball-tampering-grilling/), Marsh declines to share his account and says he has moved on. Asked why he did not sign the four frontline bowlers’ statement, he again declines to revisit his recollections. [Read his response](/documents/sp081-i-ve-moved-on-marsh-reveals-khawaja-phone-call-in-ball-tampering-grilling/#p-010). Refusing to answer is neither a denial nor an admission; the four-bowler joint denial is not Marsh’s statement.

This 12 November 2026 exchange predates Bancroft’s later detailed statement. The collected source is a complete published report with selected quotations, not the full press conference or listening-verified transcript.
