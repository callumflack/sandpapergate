## Limits and open work

The acquired original is Seven’s own published report of the memoir, not the original complete book or an unedited statement. Selected book quotations are attributed as memoir extracts only. The denial of others knowing the plot is reporter framing in p004. The direct teammeeting denial and throwing-on-bounce explanation are p014; distinguish these. Collective responsibility wording is not a specific admission of personal sandpaper knowledge. The publisher p002inaccurately compresses all three sanctions to a year; Bancroft’s ban was9months. Frozen publisher wording is retained, not endorsed or silently corrected. The prospective teammeeting wording about what we are going to do does not categorically deny a later post-exposure meeting about accepting collective responsibility.

Capture annotation for [p-002](#p-002): This report groups all three suspensions as one year. CA’s sanctions release SP005 p-018 specifies 12 months for Smith and Warner and nine months for Bancroft. Original publisher wording and paragraph ID are preserved.
