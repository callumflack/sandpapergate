## Scope limits that matter to the claims

- Cummins denies his own knowledge. His answer saying he is not sure whether more than three knew cannot be turned into a finding that only three knew. The full draft is navigation for checking that answer, not a certified quotation.
- Recollection that a team meeting did not occur and lack of recollection are different. ABC’s published quotation records Cummins saying he does not remember the collective-responsibility conversation: [SP026 · Pat Cummins’s 13 November press conference (ABC live coverage)](/documents/sp026-13-november-cummins-press-conference-abc-live-record/): [p-187](/documents/sp026-13-november-cummins-press-conference-abc-live-record/#p-187).
- Cummins’s discussion of sandpaper for bats and practice balls is not an admission of knowing it was used on a match ball. His list of methods he has heard about is not a statement that he used or witnessed each one.
- Both automatic systems can mishear the same phrase or omit quiet reporter questions. Proper names and words such as sandpaper, ball tampering and condone need particular care. Publisher text is preferred for exact argument quotations where available.
