This project assembles the major Sandpapergate documents, statements and recordings, with a timeline, participant indexes and unresolved questions. The aim is to make the evidence easier to inspect and cite. Conclusions remain the reader’s.

## Public edition

This is the public reference edition. The [ABC 7.30 interview transcript](/documents/sp024-khawaja-abc-7-30-interview/) is published in full. The archive also publishes its own [automatic transcript of Pat Cummins’s press conference](/documents/sp070-pat-cummins-full-local-automatic-recognition-unverified-asr/). Other captured text remains private; publication permissions, original recordings and extraction accuracy still require review. No accounts, chat service or external search backend are required.
