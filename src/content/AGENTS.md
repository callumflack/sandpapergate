# Question pages

- Start with the answer you would give someone who asked the question. Put it in the first paragraph under the title, in plain language.
- Say what the sources agree on. Attribute what people say and name any disagreement or uncertainty that changes the answer. If the evidence leaves the question open, say exactly where it runs out.
- Put the few passage links that support the answer immediately below it. Link to the specific paragraphs.
- Put scope, detailed evidence, competing accounts and further investigation below the short answer. Read the opening on its own and check that it still answers the title question.

This guidance applies to individual question pages. The Questions index and dossier indexes help readers find those pages.
